Drew's Election Sandbox Visualiser (DESV)

Drew's Election Sandbox Visualiser (DESV) is an interactive sandbox tool for election enthusiasts to create, customise, and share fictional election graphics, visualisations, maps, results, and Wikipedia-style infographics.

Previous election

The previous result button always comes first. Leave the previous title empty and any text matching the current button name in the title is swapped for the previous one (so 2026 becomes 2022). Parties with no previous figures are hidden in that view.

Type last election's results into the “Previous” row under each party (in the Parties section below, or by clicking a party on the graphic). Leave it empty for a new party and it shows as “new”. Change in vote share is in percentage points.

Swing is half of one side's change in vote share minus the other side's change (the standard Butler swing). It needs current and previous vote % for both.

Blocs and leaders
Parties

Left-side parties fill in from the left, right-side parties from the right, and parties with no bloc sit in the middle. Drag any party on the graphic to move it to another side or change its order.

Party bar chart

The chart follows the Vote share / Seats toggle and the year buttons on the graphic. “By ideology” uses your party list order, so drag or reorder parties to set it.
